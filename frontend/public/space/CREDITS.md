# Orbital tour textures

- Earth: [Blue Marble Next Generation, July, with topography and bathymetry](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/), NASA Earth Observatory, Reto Stöckli and the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
- Clouds: [Blue Marble cloud composite](https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg), NASA Earth Observatory. The composite is used for the orbital view separately from imagery for the selected calendar day.
- Moon: [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, using LRO/LROC imagery.
The textures were downsampled where needed and converted to WebP. The Moon's position, size, and lighting are staged for the tour.
